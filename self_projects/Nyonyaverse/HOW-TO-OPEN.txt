Nyonyaverse slides (18 slides)

HOW TO OPEN
1. Unzip this file (right-click > Extract All on Windows, double-click on Mac).
2. Open the folder "Nyonyaverse-Slides" and double-click index.html.
   It opens in Chrome, Edge or Safari. No internet and no install needed.
   Keep all the files together in the same folder.

KEYS
  Right arrow or Space : play to the next slide
  Left arrow           : play back to the previous slide
  F                    : full screen
  L                    : auto loop on / off (each slide stays 7 seconds)
  Type a number (e.g. 7): jump straight to that slide (0 = the very start)
  Move the mouse       : shows the slide-number bar, full-screen and loop buttons

ALSO INSIDE
